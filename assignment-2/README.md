\# Assignment 2: Group Food Order



I created four tables: students, menu\_items, orders, and order\_items. Primary keys identify individual records, and foreign keys connect each order item to its student, order, and menu item. I used DATE for order dates and NUMERIC for prices so the data types match the information being stored. I store the quantity and the price at the time of ordering so the bill is calculated correctly even if menu prices change later. The two queries calculate what each student owes for one order and the total for every order.
